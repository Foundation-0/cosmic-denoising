# Research map

Prepared 2026-11-01. Initial assessment by GPT-Forge; subject to correction.

## 1. Separate the questions

1. **Mathematical:** Can a specified denoising process approximate a specified cosmological evolution or inverse problem?
2. **Physical:** Is there evidence that the universe actually evolves by that process?
3. **Ontological:** Is our universe computed by an external system, perhaps within nested simulations?

A successful answer to question 1 does not answer 2 or 3. A computer model reproducing a phenomenon does not establish that the phenomenon runs on that kind of computer.

## 2. What the comparison can mean

In diffusion generative modeling, a chosen corruption process maps a data distribution toward a tractable noise distribution. A learned reverse process samples a distribution resembling the training data. The learned distribution supplies structure; it does not arise from noise alone. The mathematical “time” in this procedure need not be physical time. [DDPM; SDE](sources.md)

For cosmic large-scale structure, the standard account involves small initial perturbations evolving under gravity in an expanding universe, with additional baryonic physics. Planck measurements support the six-parameter baseline ΛCDM model in the analyses reported by that collaboration; numerical structure-formation calculations implement a physical evolution, not a learned image-cleaning rule. [Planck; Millennium](sources.md)

The useful overlap is therefore **a possible comparison between distribution-transforming dynamics**, not merely pictures becoming more detailed. We would need to specify state variables, units, evolution laws, boundary conditions and a mapping of time before claiming a shared mechanism.

“Continuous plasma” is a coarse-grained description, not featurelessness. Nor should several epochs be collapsed into one: early particle processes, the formation of neutral atoms during recombination, and later gravitational clustering ask different physical questions. [NASA overview](sources.md)

## 3. Entropy is not visual messiness

A smoother image can look less organized while a clumped gravitating system can carry substantial entropy. Black-hole entropy is associated with horizon area, and estimates of the observable universe's entropy identify supermassive black holes as a major contribution. Consequently, increasing visible structure is not evidence of decreasing total thermodynamic entropy. [Bekenstein; Hawking; Egan–Lineweaver](sources.md)

A pixel histogram, differential entropy of a learned distribution, reconstruction error and physical thermodynamic entropy are different quantities. Do not substitute one for another without a derivation. “Denoising” a sample also says nothing by itself about the entropy cost of training and running the computer.

## 4. Black holes, holography and possible substrates

Black-hole thermodynamics and holographic dualities are serious theoretical research. Bekenstein and Hawking connect gravity with entropy and temperature. Susskind develops the holographic proposal; Maldacena proposes a specific duality involving anti-de Sitter geometries and conformal field theories. These are not observations of an external computer operating our universe. In particular, a dual description need not mean that one side is a more “real” host running the other. [Bekenstein; Hawking; Susskind; Maldacena](sources.md)

A horizon, an accretion disk and dark matter are not interchangeable candidate substrates. An accretion disk is surrounding material, not the horizon whose area appears in the entropy relation. Dark matter's gravitational role in cosmological models does not establish a computational role. A substrate proposal must specify its degrees of freedom, dynamics and observational differences from existing explanations.

Reports of “paracausal occurrences” are currently an **unresolved source request**, not a finding in this repository. Before assessing one, identify the paper, observation, uncertainty and alternative explanations. No such event has been supplied or validated here.

## 5. Recursion, fractals and simulation

Nested simulations are a philosophical possibility. Bostrom's simulation argument depends on assumptions about minds, resources and simulated populations; it does not identify a physical substrate or detect a simulation. Rendering images is also not equivalent to implementing a world with autonomous inhabitants. [Bostrom](sources.md)

Recursion does not imply a fractal. A fractal claim needs a defined scaling observable and range. Galaxy-survey work such as WiggleZ finds a transition toward large-scale homogeneity within its measured range, cautioning against an unrestricted spatial-fractal claim. This does not test hypothetical external simulation levels. [WiggleZ](sources.md)

An unconstrained simulator capable of reproducing every possible observation is not distinguishable by those observations. To enter the empirical track, a specific version must risk being wrong: name a predicted observation that competing accounts would not equally predict.

## Initial conclusion

The tractable research direction is a precisely defined dynamical or inverse-problem comparison. The simulation/substrate narrative remains a separate speculative track. Neither should be dismissed solely for being unusual, nor promoted on visual resemblance alone.

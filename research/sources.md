# Sources and their roles

Initial source check: 2026-11-01. This is a reading map, not a claim of an exhaustive literature review. Links identify primary papers where available. Paper abstracts and bibliographic records were checked for this foundation; equations for any implementation must be checked against the full papers before use.

## Generative modeling

- **DDPM:** Ho, Jain and Abbeel (2020), [Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239). Primary ML research. Establishes the modeling family and training/sampling setting; does not claim a cosmological mechanism.
- **SDE:** Song et al. (2021), [Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.13456). Primary ML research. Provides forward/reverse distribution dynamics and their dependence on the score; useful for a precise mathematical comparison.

## Cosmology and structure

- **Planck:** Planck Collaboration (2020; arXiv record revised 2021), [Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209). Primary observational analysis. Supports the specified baseline cosmology, not a simulation hypothesis.
- **Millennium:** Springel et al. (2005), [Simulating the joint evolution of quasars, galaxies and their large-scale distribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0504097). Primary numerical research. Illustrates hierarchical growth through gravitational instability.
- **WiggleZ:** Scrimgeour et al. (2012), [The WiggleZ Dark Energy Survey: the transition to large-scale cosmic homogeneity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6812). Primary observational analysis. Tests spatial scaling over stated survey scales; not external simulated worlds.
- **NASA overview:** [Overview of the Universe](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/overview/). Agency explanatory reference, **not a primary research paper**. Entry point for distinguishing plasma, recombination and later structure.

## Gravity and entropy

- **Bekenstein:** Jacob D. Bekenstein (1973), [Black Holes and Entropy](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.7.2333). Primary theoretical work on horizon area, entropy and a generalized second law.
- **Hawking:** S. W. Hawking (1975), [Particle creation by black holes](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02345020). Primary semiclassical theory of black-hole thermal emission; not direct detection of an astronomical black hole's Hawking radiation.
- **Egan–Lineweaver:** Chas A. Egan and Charles H. Lineweaver (2010), [A Larger Estimate of the Entropy of the Universe](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.3983). A model-dependent entropy inventory with caveats; supports separating apparent structure from entropy.

## Holography and simulation

- **Susskind:** Leonard Susskind (1995), [The World as a Hologram](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9409089). Primary theoretical proposal motivated by quantum mechanics and gravity.
- **Maldacena:** Juan M. Maldacena (1998), [The Large N Limit of Superconformal Field Theories and Supergravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711200). Primary AdS/CFT proposal; its specified geometries and assumptions matter.
- **Bostrom:** Nick Bostrom (2003), [Are You Living in a Computer Simulation?](https://simulation-argument.com/simulation/). Original philosophical argument with explicit assumptions, not experimental physics.

## Missing evidence

No specific source has yet been identified for the motivating report about “paracausal” black-hole phenomena, an accretion-disk computing substrate, or dark matter implementing a simulation. Do not replace this gap with an invented citation.

No source listed here establishes nested simulation levels or a universal fractal ontology. Such claims remain separate from the physical and computational results the papers actually discuss.
